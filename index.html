About ELF

A Gathering for a Common Cause

The ELF Conference is a gathering of European promoters and advocates of Life and Family. It brings together organizations, activists, policymakers, and citizens from across the continent who share a common conviction: that a healthy Europe is built on the protection of human life at every stage and the strength of the family. ELF exists to connect these voices, share strategies, and build a coordinated European movement.

Our Foundation

The Three Pillars of ELF

Everything ELF stands for rests on three interconnected commitments.

E

European

ELF is a continent-wide network, bringing together advocates from across Europe to share experience, coordinate action, and speak with a unified voice in European institutions and public debate.

L

Life

Protecting the most vulnerable members of society — the unborn and the elderly — is at the heart of ELF's mission. We defend the dignity of every human life, from conception to natural death.

F

Family

The family is the natural foundation of a healthy society. ELF supports and promotes family values, advocating for policies that strengthen marriage, parenthood, and the wellbeing of children.

"A Europe worthy of its heritage is one that protects the weakest and cherishes the family."
